01 — THE IDEA

Small steps.
Bigger possibilities.

Pixel Poa is a proposed grassroots digital learning and advocacy programme designed around young people in Ndenderu, Kiambaa Constituency.

It makes technology less intimidating through practical computer skills, introductory web creation and conversations about issues that affect young people.

02 — FIND YOUR PATH

Three tracks.
One starting point.

Learning pathways are grouped by age and learning needs. Final placements and schedules will be confirmed by programme organizers.

TRACK / 01⌘
AGES 13–15

Digital Foundations

Build confidence with devices, typing, internet navigation and beginner-friendly block logic connected to learning.

  • Computer essentials
  • Digital safety
  • Foundational logic
TRACK / 02</>
AGES 16–18

Web Creators

Explore HTML, CSS and JavaScript by creating small web experiences and a starter portfolio.

  • Web structure & styling
  • Interaction basics
  • Portfolio projects
TRACK / 03▤
AGES 19–23

Digital Ready

Strengthen everyday digital confidence, productivity tools, online research and responsible digital work habits.

  • Office productivity
  • Search & research
  • Digital work readiness
